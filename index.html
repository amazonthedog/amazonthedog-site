The story of Amazon

I named my dog after the most stressful thing in my life.

I spent seven years working with Amazon sellers. Every seller had a different problem, and every one of them made me want to pull my hair out. So when a four-month-old black puppy came home on March 1, 2025, the name was obvious.

She earned it fast. From March to May she raided the trash, had accidents in the house and ate the carpet.

Then she grew up. Today she's the calmest dog in our house, patient with kids and happiest with a frisbee in her mouth. That's the dog I want you to meet.

Rob, Amazon's dad
